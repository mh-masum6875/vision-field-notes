## What this site collects

Vision Field Notes is a static website. It has no accounts and no comment system, and it does not ask you for personal information. The site search runs entirely in your browser.

## Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Cookies, advertising and analytics

If advertising or analytics are enabled on this site, third-party providers may set cookies or similar technologies:

- **Google AdSense and Google advertising partners** may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and learn how Google uses data from partner sites at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Analytics tools**, if enabled, collect aggregated information about how visitors use the site.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent notice is shown before non-essential cookies are used.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how ads are shown but not how the articles work.
